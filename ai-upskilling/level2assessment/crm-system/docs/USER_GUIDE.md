# CRM System User Guide

## 📖 **Introduction**

Welcome to the CRM System! This comprehensive Customer Relationship Management platform helps you manage customers, track sales opportunities, analyze performance, and collaborate with your team effectively.

### **Key Features**
- **Customer Management**: Complete customer lifecycle management
- **Sales Pipeline**: Track opportunities through the sales process
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time insights and performance metrics
- **AI-Powered Features**: Lead scoring and sentiment analysis
- **Real-time Notifications**: Stay updated with instant alerts
- **Role-based Access**: Secure access control for different user types

## 👥 **Customer Management**

### **Viewing Customers**

#### **Customer List**
1. Click **"Customers"** in the navigation menu
2. You'll see a table with all customers
3. Use the search bar to find specific customers
4. Filter by status, source, or assigned user
5. Sort by any column by clicking the header

#### **Customer Details**
1. Click on any customer name in the list
2. View comprehensive customer information:
   - **Basic Info**: Name, email, phone, company
   - **Status**: Lead, prospect, active, inactive
   - **Lead Score**: AI-calculated score (0-100)
   - **Notes**: Important customer information
   - **Activities**: Recent interactions and tasks
   - **Opportunities**: Associated sales opportunities

### **Adding New Customers**

#### **Step-by-Step Process**
1. Click **"Add Customer"** button
2. Fill in the required information:
   - **Name**: Customer's full name
   - **Email**: Valid email address
   - **Phone**: Contact number
   - **Company**: Organization name
   - **Status**: Current relationship stage
   - **Source**: How they found you
   - **Notes**: Additional information
3. Click **"Save"** to create the customer

#### **Best Practices**
- **Complete Information**: Fill all available fields
- **Accurate Status**: Update status as relationship progresses
- **Detailed Notes**: Add context for future reference
- **Source Tracking**: Helps with marketing analysis

### **Editing Customers**

#### **Update Information**
1. Click the **"Edit"** button next to any customer
2. Modify the information as needed
3. Click **"Save"** to update

#### **Status Management**
- **Lead**: New contact, initial interest
- **Prospect**: Qualified lead, considering purchase
- **Active**: Current customer, ongoing relationship
- **Inactive**: Past customer, no current activity

### **Customer Activities**

#### **Adding Activities**
1. Select a customer from the list
2. Click **"Add Activity"** button
3. Choose activity type:
   - **Call**: Phone conversations
   - **Meeting**: In-person or virtual meetings
   - **Email**: Email communications
   - **Task**: To-do items and follow-ups
4. Fill in details and due date
5. Click **"Save"**

#### **Managing Activities**
- **View**: See all activities for a customer
- **Edit**: Modify activity details
- **Complete**: Mark activities as done
- **Delete**: Remove unnecessary activities

## 💼 **Sales Pipeline Management**

### **Understanding the Pipeline**

#### **Sales Stages**
1. **Qualification**: Initial contact and needs assessment
2. **Proposal**: Solution presentation and pricing
3. **Negotiation**: Terms discussion and refinement
4. **Closed Won**: Deal completed successfully
5. **Closed Lost**: Opportunity not pursued

### **Creating Opportunities**

#### **Step-by-Step Process**
1. Navigate to **"Sales"** in the menu
2. Click **"Add Opportunity"** button
3. Fill in opportunity details:
   - **Title**: Descriptive opportunity name
   - **Customer**: Select from existing customers
   - **Amount**: Expected deal value
   - **Stage**: Current sales stage
   - **Probability**: Likelihood of closing (0-100%)
   - **Expected Close Date**: Target closing date
   - **Description**: Detailed opportunity information
4. Click **"Save"**

### **Managing Opportunities**

#### **Pipeline View**
- **Kanban Board**: Visual representation of opportunities by stage
- **Drag & Drop**: Move opportunities between stages
- **Quick Edit**: Click to modify opportunity details
- **Progress Tracking**: See probability and value changes

#### **Opportunity Details**
- **Basic Info**: Title, customer, amount, stage
- **Timeline**: Expected close date and milestones
- **Activities**: Related calls, meetings, tasks
- **Notes**: Important information and updates

### **Sales Analytics**

#### **Pipeline Metrics**
- **Total Value**: Sum of all opportunities
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of closed won deals
- **Average Deal Size**: Mean opportunity value
- **Sales Velocity**: Time from creation to close

#### **Performance Tracking**
- **Individual Performance**: Sales rep metrics
- **Team Performance**: Overall team statistics
- **Trend Analysis**: Historical performance data
